# Investigation Logs

## Overview

The **Investigation Logs** directory houses promoted, verified investigative case studies produced by students. These entries represent the practical application of the course's authentic performance tasks, establishing a public, permanent repository of investigative reporting and forensic debunks.

---

## Core Investigation Categories

### 1. Job Vetting Investigations (`job-vetting/`)
Formative investigations analyzing suspicious recruitment campaigns on platforms like LinkedIn and job boards.
- **Key Evidence:** Corporate registry filings, reverse search of recruiter headshots for GAN artifacts, and domain WHOIS/DNS matching.

### 2. Health Misinformation Debunks (`health-debunking/`)
Investigations tracking viral, algorithmically amplified health claims (e.g., predatory diets, dangerous medical remedies).
- **Key Evidence:** Vector tracking of viral spread, financial incentive mapping, and refutations backed by peer-reviewed clinical research.

### 3. Audiovisual & Conflict Forensics (`video-forensics/`)
Deconstructions of viral video clips circulating on platforms like Telegram, X, and TikTok during crisis events.
- **Key Evidence:** Keyframe fragmentation via InVID, shadow chronolocation (SunCalc), and satellite matching.

### 4. Summative Capstone Investigations (`capstone-projects/`)
Original, multi-vector investigative packages addressing unmet public information needs.
- **Key Evidence:** Custom Python scrapers, Google Pinpoint document analyses, permanent archive links, and triangulated reporting.

---

## Log Entry Structure

All promoted logs must include:
- **Investigative Hypothesis:** The claim or entity interrogated.
- **Triangulation Matrix:** Table summarizing at least 2 independent verification vectors.
- **Archival Chain of Custody:** Cryptographic hashes or permanent archive snapshots.
- **Contributor Attribution:** Student name, term, and link to personal workbench repository.
