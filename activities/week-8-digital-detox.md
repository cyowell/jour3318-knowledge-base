## Purpose & Context

In the first seven weeks of this course, you moved from theoretical analysis of Information Disorder (Phase 1) to the applied mechanics of visual forensics, reverse searching, EXIF extraction, and geolocation (Phase 2).

During this work, you explored how digital media is weaponized: how emotional appeals bypass critical scrutiny, how synthetic images deceive human perception, and how algorithms reward high-arousal, unverified content.

Week 8 provides an intentional pause — a **digital silence week** — allowing you to step away from screen saturation, observe your own media habits with journalistic detachment, and synthesize what you have learned before diving into computational journalism (Python) in Week 9.

---

## The Detox Protocol

1. **Screen-Free Window:** Set aside dedicated periods of complete disconnection from algorithmic feeds (social media, short-form video, breaking news notifications).
2. **Observation Journal:** Observe moments when you reflexively reach for your phone or seek dopamine-driven stimulation. Note the triggers (boredom, anxiety, curiosity, habit).
3. **Cognitive Audit:** Contrast how you consumed digital content before this course with the verification lens you now apply.

## Reflection Prompt & Guiding Questions

Write a 750–1,000 word metacognitive reflection addressing the following four areas:

### 1. Algorithmic Habit Analysis
- What did you observe about your personal scrolling loops and digital consumption patterns during the detox window?
- Which specific platform mechanics (infinite scroll, personalized recommendations, push alerts) do you find most manipulative of your attention?

### 2. Cognitive Recalibration
- How has learning about Frankfurt's *On Bullshit* and Wardle's *Taxonomy of Information Disorder* changed the way you process breaking news or viral claims?
- Can you identify a specific instance during the past seven weeks where you paused to verify a claim that you previously might have accepted at face value?

### 3. The Human Dimension of Visual Verification
- Having practiced reverse image searches, EXIF inspection, and InVID keyframe analysis, how do you view the relationship between visual media and truth?
- Why do you think human beings remain susceptible to visual deception even when forensic tools exist to debunk it?

### 4. Looking Ahead to Computational Tools
- As we transition from manual OSINT into Python scripting and AI document analysis, what questions or concerns do you have about the growing role of automation in public information ecosystems?
