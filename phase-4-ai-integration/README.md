# Phase 4: Deep AI Integration & Original Creation

## Overview

Phase 4 bridges advanced artificial intelligence systems with investigative reporting. Students explore how machine learning models can accelerate document synthesis, pattern detection across massive public archives, and forensic analysis, while exercising uncompromising editorial oversight to detect AI hallucinations and bias.

---

## Core Focus Areas

- **Google Pinpoint & Large-Document Forensics:** Indexing, searching, optical character recognition (OCR), and entity extraction across thousands of pages of unstructured public records and FOIA releases.
- **Global Event Database Interrogation (GDELT):** Querying and visualizing global news trends, conflict mapping, and media framing patterns.
- **Synthetic Provenance & Watermarking:** Evaluating AI provenance systems, including Google DeepMind's SynthID, frequency domain watermark detection, and C2PA content credentials.
- **Algorithmic Auditing & Hallucination Forensics:** Documenting systematic failures, historical biases, and fabricated citations produced by large language models.
- **Custom AI Tooling (Google AI Studio):** Building specialized system prompts, structured Gemini Gems, and API-assisted analysis pipelines.
